About Candy Dandy

This has been the project for our Final Exam at Mediacollege Amsterdam. It involves both Game Development and Game Artist course with different criteria to be met. In response to our external clients assignment to create a innovative match-3 metagame in which, the match-3 mechanic has direct influence onto the secondary gameplay layer or game. Our client being Ramses Di Perna besides being a Game Dev at Azerion also an old student at our college.

Intro

At the start of this project there was a briefing by 3 external clients. We as students with our group could communicate our preferences. In the end we got our nr. 1 pick. So we approached our client and asked a few questions and exchanged contact info to update each them. On the first day of the official exam our group gathered together and scanned our intel to come up with ideas. Almost everybody had their own ideas, some indifferent some alike until we had 3 core concepts. Some folks wanted to make a dating sim, the other a cake baking sim and me and the other 2 devs wanted a fighting game. So we eventually convinced them and decided to go for a fighting game. Then we had difference of opinion within the group. About how, some people thought it was better to make landscape orientated game over a portrait because it would be too cropped and small. So we had 1 of the artists draw a mockup of the 2 concepts we were divided about and presented the question with context to our client. Also forgot to mention, the reason we devs didn't agree with the dating sim and cake baking sim concepts because they seemed art heavy to such a degree, we wouldn't have much more to do outside of the Core mechanics required and the short amount of time we could create those.

Concept 1: Portrait style matching and fighting simultaneously
Concept 2: Landscape style matching utilizing full screen -> Transition into fighting cutscene based off matches made.

Development

The chosen concept ended up being Concept 1 due to it fitting the briefing better. Well starting off this development cycle, the artists took roles and we devs mainly set up the trello with userstories and a main focus on the core mechanics I started on the Character classes and their hierarchy. Our game would be centered around PvE combat so we needed a Player and an Enemy so i created the class Character.cs, and decided to use inheritance so that both the player and enemy characters would be provided with base properties such as the ability to attack, health and die.
