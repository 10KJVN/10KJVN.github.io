## Concept

<Split ratio="2:3" align="center">
    <div>

        First Spark is a short experimental game inspired by the concept of Homo Ludens — "the playing human."
        The game invites players to explore without explicit instructions, relying purely on instinct and environmental cues.

        You begin as a primitive human inside a dark cave, your fire extinguished.
        The world is obscured in shadow and mist, pushing you to move forward guided only by faint glimmers of light.
        This gradual transition — from almost complete darkness to a blindingly bright finale — is intentional, reflecting the idea of finding the "light" within yourself.

        *Trigger warning: volume might be loud.*

    </div>

## Design Pillars

The concept was born from the metaphor of losing something essential and instinctively searching to reclaim it — just as ancient humans would guard their fire.
This primal urge is paired with modern design sensibilities to create a layered, symbolic experience.

<Split ratio="2:3" align="center">
    <div>

        ### Environmental level design

        Instead of tutorials or UI markers, the game uses light, shadow, and subtle visual hints to guide the player.

    </div>

## Technical Work

<Split ratio="1:1">
    <div>

        ### Build

        - Designed dynamic lighting setups to subtly lead the player's movement.
        - Created and tuned volumetric fog to evoke mystery and depth.
        - Built the game loop with a simple timer and entity collection system, with restart/quit functionality for quick replayability.

    </div>
    <div>

        ### Research & inspiration

        - Studied environmental storytelling to remove the need for tutorials.
        - Experimented with Unity's lighting and fog systems to balance atmosphere with performance.

    </div>
</Split>

## Reflection

First Spark is aimed at players who enjoy atmospheric, symbolic experiences and are willing to explore without being told exactly what to do.

<Split ratio="2:3" align="center">
    <div>

        If I had more time, I would:

        - Add a main menu and more refined transitions.
        - Create disappearance effects for the mist entities.
        - Expand the playable area with additional content tied to the theme.

    </div>
    <div>

        First Spark is a small but complete prototype — museum-worthy in its symbolism and design intent — and a personal exploration of how play, instinct, and meaning intersect.

    </div>
</Split>
