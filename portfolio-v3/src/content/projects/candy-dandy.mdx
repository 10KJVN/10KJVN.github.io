## Overview

<Split ratio="1:1" align="center">
    <div>

        Candy Dandy was our final exam project at `Mediacollege`, created in collaboration with `Azerion`.
        The goal: develop a match-3 mobile game where matching tiles directly affects a secondary gameplay layer.
        The team was 3 developers and 5 artists.

        My features:

        - Designed and implemented [Character Classes](https://github.com/j5x/PvB2025/tree/develop/Assets/Script/CharacterSystem)

        - Handled Canvas & UI scaling for mobile ([Safe Area Handler](https://github.com/j5x/PvB2025/blob/develop/Assets/Script/UI/SafeArea.cs))
        - Integrated [VFX](https://github.com/j5x/PvB2025/tree/develop/Assets/Script/Vfx) and optimized textures

        Builds are available for `Android` and `Windows`.
        Since the game was designed for portrait mobile, expect stretched visuals on PC.

## Project Kickoff

The project began with three external client pitches.
After securing our first choice, we brainstormed concepts.
Options ranged from a dating sim to a cake-baking sim, but we pushed for a fighting game with match-3 integration.
Early mockups helped us and the client decide on a portrait layout to balance scope and usability.

## Mobile UI & Safe Area

A major challenge was scaling across devices.
Using Unity's Canvas Scaler and a custom Safe Area Handler, I ensured UI panels adapt to notches, rounded corners, tablets, and Android soft keys.
This made the game consistently playable across a wide range of devices.
