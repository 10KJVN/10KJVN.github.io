About Godspeed

The first UE5 project I worked on alongside Veldboom Studios. Initially conceived as an experimental racing prototype, the project became a year-long experiment for me.

Veldboomstudios.com

My Contributions:

  • Vehicle Gameplay (AI & Manual)
  • PCG (Procedural Generation)
  • Game Systems
  • UI/UX

Intro

From AI driven cars, PCG props, multiple game modes, to recycled quest and garage systems. Constantly changing at a godspeed rate, things like these make the knowledge gained so great

Development

Early Development (July-Dec 2023):
Learned Unreal's workflow through a multitude of tutorial series and official documentation.
By applying the techniques showcased I, started understanding blueprints through trial and error.



MVP Release (June 2024):
By June, we shipped the first MVP release (V0.0.01) with one track, one car, checkpoints, and a working lap timer. This marked the first time the game loop was complete from start to finish. Also, custom exclusion logic for the PCG. Since the trees are placed through the Biome system


Released: June 9, 2024 - Relevant Gameplay; 1:10 ~ 4:50


Second Release (July 2024):
By July, we had a second release (V0.0.02) featuring:

- Third-person game mode.
- Garage system modularized into the pit lane.
- General bugfixes and stability improvements.



Latest Release: July 19, 2024. Relevant Gameplay; 11:50 ~ 16:30

On Hold (August 2024):
Focus shifted towards polishing vehicle handling, refining UI/UX, and planning features like multiple vehicles and extended track systems. Development paused after August 2024 as priorities shifted to new projects.
