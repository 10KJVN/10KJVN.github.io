About Godspeed

Godspeed is the first UE5 project I worked on, developed as a personal initiative alongside the Veldboom Studios team. It was envisioned as an experimental racing game prototype where I explored the engine's systems, collaborative workflows, and game development practices outside of Unity. VeldboomStudios.com

My Contributions:

  • Procedural Generation
  • Vehicle Gameplay
  • Game Systems
  • Version Control

Intro

The project started in July 2023 and became a year-long experiment in learning Unreal’s landscape tools, procedural systems, and packaging workflow. It was never intended as a commercial release, but instead as a space to learn, experiment, and collaborate.


First Release: June 9, 2024. Relevant Gameplay; 1:10 ~ 4:50


Latest Release: July 19, 2024. Relevant Gameplay; 11:50 ~ 16:30

Development

The development journey went through several key stages:

Early Exploration (July 2023 – December 2023):
Learned Unreal’s workflow, landscape tools, and RVT (Runtime Virtual Texture) research.
Built early terrain, road systems, and experimented with garage/game mode concepts.

During early development I mainly worked on AI driven cars based on: CLM's tutorial series. Due to an upcoming showcase I had 7 days to implement as much as I could. Resulting in: Collision Avoidance, Enter/Exit Road, Left/Right and 2 way road driving, adaptize speed and Predictive Steering.

First Playable & MVP (January – June 2024):
By June 9, 2024, we shipped the first MVP release (V0.0.01) with one track, one car, checkpoints, and a working lap timer. This marked the first time the game loop was complete from start to finish. Also custom exclusion logic for the PCG. Since the trees are placed through the Biome system

Second Release & Garage Integration (July 2024):
By July 23, 2024, we had a second release (V0.0.02) featuring:

- Third-person camera mode.
- Garage system integrated into the pit lane.
- General bugfixes and stability improvements.


Polishing & Roadmap (August 2024 – On Hold):
Focus shifted towards polishing vehicle handling, refining UI/UX, and planning features like multiple vehicles and extended track systems. Development paused after August 2024 as priorities shifted to new projects.
